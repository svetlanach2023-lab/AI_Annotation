# Ukrainian Financial Documents

Real-world Ukrainian accounting and tax documents annotated for AI training.

This dataset focuses on primary financial records commonly used in Ukrainian businesses, including tax invoices, acts of completed works, and supporting documents.

**Key Characteristics**
- 110 annotation classes
- Real business documents from operational workflows
- Emphasis on tabular data, legal entities, financial calculations and compliance elements

**Objective**  
To enable AI models to accurately parse and understand Ukrainian financial documentation for automation, compliance and reporting tasks.
