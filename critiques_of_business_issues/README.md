# Model Critiques

Structured evaluations of Grok AI responses on business, financial and operational topics.

This collection demonstrates critical analysis of AI outputs, focusing on factual accuracy, logical consistency, business relevance and practical usefulness.

**Content**
- Prompt + AI response + detailed critique
- Identification of hallucinations and reasoning gaps
- Actionable improvement suggestions

**Objective**  
To contribute to the alignment and quality improvement of AI models in professional business contexts.
