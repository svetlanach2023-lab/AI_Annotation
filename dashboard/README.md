# Business Intelligence Dashboards

Annotated collection of real Power BI and Excel dashboards used in operational and financial management.

This dataset captures complex visualization layouts, KPI cards, interactive filters, and analytical charts that support business decision-making.

**Focus Areas**
- Chart interpretation and data relationships
- KPI card extraction
- Filter and slicer mechanics
- Dashboard hierarchy and visual storytelling

**Objective**  
To train AI systems to read, interpret and extract insights from modern business intelligence interfaces.
