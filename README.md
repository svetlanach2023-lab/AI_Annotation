# AI Annotation Portfolio

High-precision annotated datasets and structured model critiques developed for training computer vision and large language models in real-world business, financial and geospatial domains.

### Datasets

**[Ukrainian Financial Documents](https://github.com/svetlanach2023-lab/AI_Annotation/blob/main/invoices_Ukraine/README.md)**  
Real operational documents from Ukrainian enterprises.  
• **110 annotation classes**  
• **~150 annotated images** with complex layouts  
• Includes tax invoices (НН), acts of completed works, accounting references, seals, signatures, tabular sections, EDRPOU codes, and financial calculations  
• Focus: tabular data extraction, entity recognition, compliance validation

**[European Invoices](https://github.com/svetlanach2023-lab/AI_Annotation/blob/main/Invoice/README.md)**  
Real Estonian import invoices from international trade transactions.  
• **129 annotation classes**  
• Multi-country commercial format  
• Emphasis on European invoice structure, SWIFT codes, VAT details, beneficiary information and cross-border financial terminology

**[Business Intelligence Dashboards](https://github.com/svetlanach2023-lab/AI_Annotation/blob/main/dashboard/README.md)**  
Collection of real management dashboards built in Power BI and Excel.  
• **54 annotation classes**  
• KPI cards, trend charts, slicers, filters, visual hierarchy and storytelling elements  
• Designed to train AI in understanding modern business intelligence interfaces and extracting actionable insights

**[Odesa Region Geospatial Map](https://github.com/svetlanach2023-lab/AI_Annotation/blob/main/map_Odesa/README.md)**  
Detailed administrative map of Odesa Oblast.  
• **7 classes** (districts and key territorial units)  
• High-accuracy boundary annotation  
• Purpose: geospatial recognition and regional territorial analysis

**[Model Critiques](https://github.com/svetlanach2023-lab/AI_Annotation/blob/main/critiques_of_business_issues/README.md)**  
In-depth structured critiques of Grok AI responses on business, financial, operational and strategic topics.  
• Format: Prompt → Model Output → Detailed Critique  
• Covers hallucination detection, logical inconsistencies, business accuracy, completeness and practical usefulness  
• Provides specific improvement recommendations

### Technical Specifications
- **Annotation Tool**: CVAT (Computer Vision Annotation Tool)
- **Output Format**: YOLO (bounding boxes + class labels)
- **Quality Assurance**: Inter-annotator agreement checks and consistency validation
- **Total Classes**: 300+
- **Annotation Style**: Pixel-precise, business-context aware, production-grade

### Purpose and Business Value
This portfolio demonstrates the ability to create **high-quality, domain-specific training data** that bridges real enterprise processes with AI model improvement. All datasets are built from actual business documents and operational materials, ensuring relevance for document understanding, financial automation, dashboard interpretation and geospatial tasks.

- Advanced Data Labeling & Annotation
- Quality Control & Validation
- Domain Knowledge Translation into Training Data
- Model Critique & AI Alignment
- Creation of production-ready datasets for LLM and CV models
